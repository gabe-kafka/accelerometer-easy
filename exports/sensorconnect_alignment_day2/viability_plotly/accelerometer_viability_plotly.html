1. Three-axis source data

Lord channels are in g. Thingy channels are raw interpolated ADXL counts on the aligned SensorConnect time base.

2. Combined acceleration magnitude

Magnitude removes sensitivity to axis misalignment: sqrt(x^2 + y^2 + z^2). This plot shows Lord magnitude in g and Thingy raw magnitude in counts on separate axes before calibration.

3. Thingy raw magnitude calibrated to Lord g

Linear calibration: Lord_mag_g = 0.0002791767 * Thingy_mag_counts + -0.063018.

4. Residual error

Residual is calibrated Thingy magnitude minus Lord magnitude.

5. FFT viability comparison

FFT uses mean-removed magnitude with a Hann window. Frequency display is limited to 0-12.5 Hz, the Nyquist limit of the original 25 Hz Thingy stream.
